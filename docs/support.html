Brelly Support

Brelly shows you the weather for the places on your plan for the day, so you know which stops need an umbrella.

Get in touch

Bug reports, questions and feature requests all go to the same place: open an issue on GitHub. You'll need a free GitHub account to post one.

Please include your device model and iOS or Android version — it makes a bug much faster to track down. Issues are public, so leave out anything personal.

Common questions

Why is the forecast different from other weather apps?
Inside Singapore, Brelly uses the National Environment Agency's own forecasts via data.gov.sg. Outside Singapore, it uses Open-Meteo. Other apps use other sources, so they will not always agree.
Why didn't I get a rain alert?
Check that notifications are allowed for Brelly in your device settings, that the stop is not muted, and that it is marked as outdoors — indoor stops do not get rain alerts.
How do I move my plans to a new phone?
Link a Google or Apple account in the app on both devices and your plans will sync. You can also export your plans to a file and import it on the new device.
Why does Brelly want my calendar?
Only so you can turn an existing event into a plan, and so your plans can show up in your calendar alongside everything else. It is optional, and you can say no and use the app normally.
How do I delete my data?
Delete your plans and routines in the app, or open an issue asking for your account to be deleted. See the privacy policy for details.
