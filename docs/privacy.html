Brelly Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 August 2026

Brelly shows you the weather for the places on your plan for the day. This page explains what the app collects, where it goes, and how to get rid of it.

Brelly is made by an independent developer. There is no advertising in the app, no analytics or tracking SDK, and none of your data is sold or shared with data brokers.

What Brelly stores about you

Everything below is created by you using the app. Brelly does not ask for your name, your address, or your phone number.

  • Your plans. For each stop you add: the label you gave it, the place name, its latitude and longitude, the start and end time, any notes you typed, and whether it is indoors or outdoors.
  • Your routines. Repeating plans you have set up, and the days they apply to.
  • Your settings. App preferences, such as how far ahead you want rain alerts.
  • An account identifier. Brelly signs you in anonymously the first time you open it, which produces a random ID with no personal details attached. If you choose to link a Google or Apple account so your plans follow you to another device, that sign-in also gives Brelly the email address associated with the account you picked.

Where it is stored

Your plans, routines and settings are saved on your device and synced to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), which Brelly uses as its backend. They are stored under your account identifier and are readable only by that account. Google processes this data as Brelly's service provider — see the Firebase Privacy and Security page.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Location. Used while you are using the app, to prefill the location when you add a plan and to show nearby weather when you have no plans. Your device location is used to look up a forecast; it is not stored on Brelly's servers as a location history.
  • Calendar. Only if you turn it on. Brelly reads your calendar so you can turn an event into a plan, and can write your plans back so they appear alongside your other events. Calendar data is read on your device. Nothing from your calendar is uploaded except what becomes a plan you saved, which is then stored as described above.
  • Notifications. Used to send you a rain alert before a stop. Alerts are scheduled locally on your device.

Services Brelly sends requests to

To show a forecast and to let you search for a place, the app sends requests to these services. Each request includes what it needs to answer — a place you typed, or a set of coordinates.

  • Google Places and Google Maps — place search and autocomplete when you add a stop. (Privacy policy)
  • data.gov.sg / NEA — weather forecasts inside Singapore. (Privacy statement)
  • Open-Meteo — weather forecasts outside Singapore. (Terms and privacy)

Children

Brelly is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Getting your data out, and deleting it

You can export your plans and routines to a file from inside the app at any time, and import them again.

To delete your data, delete your plans and routines in the app, or ask for your account to be deleted using the contact link below and it will be removed from Firestore. Deleting the app from your device removes the local copy; if you have linked an account, ask for deletion so the synced copy goes too.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a deletion request: open an issue on GitHub.

GitHub issues are public, so please don't include personal details in one. If you are asking for your data to be deleted, say so without posting anything you would rather keep private, and you'll be told what is needed.
